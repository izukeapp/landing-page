Everything you need

Built for real runners

Every feature is designed around how runners actually think, train, and connect.

Discover Nearby Runners

Swipe through runners near you filtered by pace, run type, availability, and goals. Find your perfect match in minutes.

Real-time Chat

Message your matches instantly. See when they're online and plan your next run without leaving the app.

Plan Runs Together

Send run invites with date, time, location, and distance. Accept with one tap and get reminders before the run.

Running Journal

Log every run with distance, duration, and how you felt. Track your progress and celebrate milestones over time.

Moments

After every shared run, create a branded Moment card to celebrate it. Save it to your journal and share on social media.

Smart Matching

Our algorithm considers your pace range, preferred run types, availability windows, and location to surface your best matches first.
